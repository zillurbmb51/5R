Privacy Policy — 5R and Prosper

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how 5R and Prosper, developed by Zillur Rahman, handle information when you use the apps. Features and advertising services may differ by app, platform, and version.

Information Handled by the Apps

Prosper stores quiz progress, results, missed questions, and appearance preferences locally on your device.

5R stores game settings and progress locally. Its online features use Google Firebase to process an anonymous account identifier, your chosen player name, scores, online status, game activity, and chat messages. Player names, scores, and online status may be visible to other players; messages are shared with participants in the relevant game. Avoid including sensitive personal information in player names or messages.

If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond to your request.

Advertising

The apps use Google AdMob where advertising is enabled. Prosper on iOS also includes Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation. Prosper’s current Android version displays advertising placeholders.

Advertising providers may process information such as IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information. This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and personalize advertising where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Your Advertising Choices

Use Ad privacy options in the app to access the advertising privacy choices available to you. Available options depend on your region and the advertising provider’s configuration. Where applicable, you can change or withdraw consent through this form.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements or stop processing needed to deliver permitted ads.
